# 0020: Narration for the demo videos

- **Status:** Accepted for the prototype
- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Deciders:** repo owner (asked for voice-overs, then for a British voice); built in Claude Code

## Context

The owner asked: "lets also add audio/voice overs to the demo video", and then: "Lets make the voice over british".

The six recordings on the site are made by script from the preview (`apps/site/scripts/record-demos.mjs`). Each already has a caption bar that says what is happening, and the captions are printed under each video as a text list. They had no sound. Two things shape how sound is added. The recordings are made again whenever the tool changes, so the narration has to be made again by the same script, not by hand. And the build guide asks before a paid connector or licence is added, and nothing about the project is sent to an outside service without the owner saying so.

## Options considered

1. **A paid voice service through a connector.** The best voices. It costs credits on the owner's account for every regeneration, sends the script of each video to the service, and can only be run from a session that has the connector, so `record-demos.mjs` could not make a narrated recording by itself.
2. **A person records the lines.** The most natural result. It cannot be regenerated by script, and it puts a real person's voice in a repository that holds synthetic material only.
3. **An open-source voice that runs on the machine making the recordings (chosen).** No cost, nothing leaves the machine, and the recorder makes the sound itself. The voice is good and plainly computer-generated, which the page says.

## Decision

- **The words are the captions.** Each caption is spoken as it is written, so what is heard, what is on the caption bar and what is in the text list under the video are the same sentence. A few terms are given to the voice in their spoken form (`narration.config.json`, `spoken`): EBITDA and SHA-256.
- **The voice** is `bf_emma`, British English, from the Kokoro model (version 1.0, Apache 2.0 licence) run with `kokoro-onnx` 0.6.1 (MIT licence) on `onnxruntime`. It is the British voice the model's own voice list grades highest. The voice, the pace and the model files with their SHA-256 values are in `apps/site/narration.config.json`; a model file that does not match its pinned value is refused.
- **Timing.** A caption stays on screen until it has been said, plus a short gap, so the voice never runs into the next caption. The recorder speaks the captions of the last run before the camera rolls; a caption that is new is timed by an estimate, and its recording is made a second time if the clip turns out longer than the room it was given. A narrated recording may run to a minute and a half (it was a minute).
- **The file.** The clips are laid under the picture at the times their captions appear and written as one Opus track (mono, 48 kb/s) in the same WebM file. The picture is copied, not encoded again. A recording gains about 0.3 MB.
- **What is committed.** The recordings and the manifest, as before. The clips and the model files (about 350 MB) are kept in `apps/site/.cache`, which is ignored.
- **What the page says.** The section says that a computer-generated voice reads each caption aloud, and each video says "narrated" beside its length. Nothing plays by itself.
- **Without the tools.** `--no-narration` records as before. Narration needs `uv` (the script lists its own dependency) and an ffmpeg that can write Opus, which the one Playwright installs cannot.

## Consequences

- The site's videos have sound. A viewer who cannot hear it loses nothing: the captions are in the picture and in the text list.
- Regenerating the videos takes longer (about a second of computing for each second of speech the first time; cached afterwards) and needs the model download once.
- The voice has not been heard by a person in this session: it was checked by measurement (each line starts within a tenth of a second of its caption; no line overlaps the next; loudness and peak level) and by reading the phonemes the engine produces for the finance terms. The owner should listen and say if the voice, the pace or a pronunciation should change; each is one line of configuration.
- Changing to another voice of the model, or to a paid service later, changes `lib/narration.mjs` only; the recorder and the page do not care where a clip came from.

## Versions checked

- `kokoro-onnx` 0.6.1 (PyPI), its `Kokoro.create(text, voice, speed, lang)` read from the installed source on 2026-11-12.
- Model files `kokoro-v1.0.onnx` and `voices-v1.0.bin` from the `model-files-v1.0` release of the `kokoro-onnx` project; voice grades from the model's `VOICES.md`, read on 2026-11-12.
